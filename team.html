Our Healthcare Professionals

Our friendly and professional team is dedicated to your family's health and well-being. We believe in building lasting relationships with our patients and providing care that treats you like family.

Teddy Phillips, FNP-BC

Family Nurse Practitioner & Founder

Ted Phillips is a board-certified Family Nurse Practitioner and the founder of Phillips Family Healthcare. He specializes in comprehensive family medicine, managing everything from routine wellness visits to chronic conditions.

Patients appreciate Ted's compassionate approach and the time he takes to listen to their concerns, ensuring personalized care for each individual. His philosophy centers on treating patients like family and building long-term healthcare relationships.

Specialties: Family Medicine, Chronic Disease Management, Preventive Care, Pediatrics

Our Wound Care Partners

Phillips Family Healthcare partners with Precision Wound Management, a specialized wound care team, to provide advanced wound care services on-site. This collaboration allows our patients to receive expert wound treatment without leaving our clinic.

Dr. April Carter, MD, MHA, FACP

Medical Director of Precision Wound Management

Dr. Carter brings a wealth of experience in internal medicine and leads our wound care program with a commitment to the highest standards of care. She provides clinical oversight and develops evidence-based treatment protocols for complex wound cases.

Credentials: Doctor of Medicine, Master of Healthcare Administration, Fellow of the American College of Physicians

Aldridge Westrich, APRN

Nurse Practitioner, Wound Care Specialist

Aldridge works closely with Dr. Carter to deliver hands-on treatment and education to patients. He has extensive experience in wound assessment and management, ensuring patients receive compassionate, effective care at each visit.

Specialties: Advanced Wound Assessment, Debridement, Patient Education, Wound Care Protocols
