Precision Wound Management

Advanced Wound Care, Right at Our Clinic

Phillips Family Healthcare is proud to offer specialized wound care through our in-house Precision Wound Management program. This physician-led team provides expert, evidence-based treatment for acute and chronic wounds, helping patients heal faster while staying close to home.

"Precision Wound Management is dedicated to providing expert, evidence-based care for acute and chronic wounds. Our mission is to accelerate healing, reduce complications, and restore quality of life through precise clinical intervention and compassionate service."

Dr. April Carter, MD, MHA, FACP

Medical Director of Precision Wound Management

Dr. Carter brings a wealth of experience in internal medicine and leads our wound care program with a commitment to the highest standards of care. She provides clinical oversight and develops evidence-based treatment protocols for complex wound cases.

Aldridge Westrich, APRN

Nurse Practitioner, Wound Care Specialist

Aldridge works closely with Dr. Carter to deliver hands-on treatment and education to patients. He has extensive experience in wound assessment and management, ensuring patients receive compassionate, effective care at each visit.

Wound Care Services Include:

  • Advanced wound assessment and monitoring
  • Debridement and specialized dressings
  • Infection management and antibiotic therapy coordination
  • Personalized wound care education for patients and caregivers
  • Collaboration with primary providers and specialists
