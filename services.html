Our Services

Comprehensive healthcare services for you and your entire family

Healthcare Services

At Phillips Family Healthcare, we offer a wide range of primary and specialized healthcare services to meet your needs. Our goal is to provide quality, compassionate care for every member of your family.

Primary Care & Wellness

Our primary care services form the foundation of your family's health. We focus on preventive care and early detection to help you stay healthy.

  • Annual wellness exams and physicals
  • Preventive health screenings
  • Immunizations and vaccinations
  • Health risk assessments
  • Acute illness treatment (cold, flu, infections)
  • Minor injury care

Women's Health

We provide comprehensive women's health services tailored to every stage of life.

  • Annual wellness exams
  • Pap smears and cervical screenings
  • Breast exams
  • Contraception counseling
  • Menopause management
  • Hormone therapy consultations

Pediatric Care

From newborns to teenagers, we provide caring and thorough healthcare for your children.

  • Well-child visits and developmental screenings
  • Childhood immunizations
  • School and sports physicals
  • Treatment for common childhood illnesses
  • Growth and nutrition counseling
  • Behavioral health support

Chronic Disease Management

We partner with you to manage ongoing health conditions and improve your quality of life.

  • Diabetes management and education
  • Hypertension monitoring and treatment
  • High cholesterol management
  • Thyroid disorder management
  • Asthma and COPD care
  • Heart disease prevention and management

Weight Management

Achieve your health goals with personalized weight management support.

  • Individualized weight loss plans
  • Nutritional counseling
  • Lifestyle modification guidance
  • Medical weight management options
  • Ongoing support and monitoring

Lab & Diagnostic Testing

Convenient on-site testing for faster results and quicker treatment decisions.

  • Blood work and lab panels
  • Glucose and A1C testing
  • Cholesterol screenings
  • Urinalysis
  • Rapid strep and flu tests
  • COVID-19 testing

Specialized Wound Care
